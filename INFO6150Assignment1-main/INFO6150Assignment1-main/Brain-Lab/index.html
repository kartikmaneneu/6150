DO YOU KNOW?

The human brain is the most complex organ in the body, containing approximately 86 billion neurons that communicate through trillions of synapses. It is the control center for all bodily functions and is responsible for thought, memory, emotion, and behavior. Sometimes, the brain can surprise us with its incredible abilities and mysteries. Also, the brain has the ability to rewire itself throughout a person's life, a concept known as neuroplasticity.

🧠 The Sleep Cycle

Stage Duration Key Function
N1 (Light Sleep) 1 - 7 minutes Transition to sleep
N2 (Slightly Deeper) 10 - 25 minutes Body temperature drops
N3 (Deep Sleep) 20 - 40 minutes Physical recovery
REM (Rapid Eye Movement) 10 - 60 minutes Dreaming & memory consolidation
Sleep Spindles 1 - 5 minutes Memory strengthening & learning
Wake After Sleep Onset 1 - 10 minutes Brief alertness before returning to sleep
Slow-Wave Sleep Peak 30 - 60 minutes Restoration, immune support & hormone regulation
